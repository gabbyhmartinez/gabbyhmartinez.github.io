Coding Challenge 3: Policy debate

Chart 5: Drug use and mental health disorders

I focused on mental health disorders and substance abuse. People with mental health disorders are often more prone to substance abuse, so some argue policy should address the two together.

This chart shows how much more likely drug dependency is given a specific mental health disorder. The increased risk is at minimum one, and at maximum over six times higher. The data suggests it is important to tackle drug dependency in combination with mental health policy.

Coding Challenge 3: Policy debate

Chart 6: Exploring death rates

Death rates for substance abuse and mental health disorders are much higher in North America and Europe than in other regions. These regions may benefit even more from a policy approach that tackles the two issues together, given the real societal impact.

Coding Challenge 4: Replication

Chart 8: Recreation

The biggest change is the x axis. Instead of printing the population next to each bar, I show it in millions along the axis in steps of 5 million, with a vertical grid so each city is easy to place. I also added more space between bars and between titles and data, because the original felt slightly cluttered, and a tooltip so you can hover over a bar to see the exact population.
