Prosperity in the UK and Glasgow City Region

Local Economy · LSE PP422 Final Project · March 2026 · Python, Predictive Modeling, Policy Analysis

Project Team: Sophia Bucklin, Meera Burghardt, Gabriella Martinez, Sophie Sfeir

Overview

Analyzed the distribution, specialization, and growth of eight priority UK industrial sectors (IS8) across approximately 350 local authorities, using employment and business data to identify regional patterns associated with frontier-industry growth. Findings were used to inform predictive modeling and policy recommendations for the Glasgow City Region specifically.

• Developed and analyzed measures of IS8 employment share, employment levels and growth, and business composition and growth to assess the importance and evolution of frontier industries across UK local authorities.

• Conducted national exploratory analysis in Python, including geographic analysis, sector-level comparisons, growth analysis, and location quotient (LQ) analysis to identify areas of regional specialization.

• Applied K-means clustering to location quotients to identify local authorities with similar patterns of industrial specialization.

• Contributed national outcome measures and analysis to the project's predictive modeling, using IS8 employment share as the primary measure of regional specialization.

• Applied findings to the Glasgow City Region and used counterfactual analysis to estimate 60,000+ potential additional jobs from addressing barriers and 160,000+ jobs at risk from losing existing strengths.

• Translated quantitative findings into policy recommendations focused on sector collaboration, Glasgow's university ecosystem, and health disparities.
